# Asset credits

Every image, sprite sheet, tileset, sound effect and music loop in this guide was **generated by
code** written for the guide:

- pictures: `tools/make_images.ts` (drawn with the browser's canvas)
- sounds and music: `tools/make_audio.ts` (synthesised from simple waves and noise)

They are free to use, change and share, in coursework and anywhere else, with no attribution
required. If you use art from anywhere else, check its licence and credit its author - "found it on
the internet" is not a licence.
